# Issue 018: The Map Captain

## Status
Draft Complete

## Inspiration Seed
Ali at Khaybar: responsibility and courage with humility.

## Invisible Moral Pattern
An underestimated child is trusted with guidance and leads carefully rather than proudly.

## Child-World Transformation
On a hill trail, the teacher gives the map to a quiet child who helps the group finish safely.

## Main Moral Value
Trustworthiness.

## Secondary Value
Responsible courage.

## Suggested Setting
Hill trail.

## Main Character
Owen, age 9, quiet, careful, and secretly good at maps.

## Supporting Characters
Teacher, louder classmates, walking group.

## Natural Humour Idea
A classmate keeps holding the map upside down with total confidence.

## Moral Problem
Owen must lead without showing off or shrinking back.

## Resolution Direction
He uses the map patiently, asks for help when needed, and guides the group.
