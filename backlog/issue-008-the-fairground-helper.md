# Issue 008: The Fairground Helper

## Status
Draft Complete

## Inspiration Seed
Abu Talha shielding someone vulnerable: protective courage.

## Invisible Moral Pattern
An older child steps away from fun to protect a smaller child in a crowded place.

## Child-World Transformation
At a school fair, a young child is pushed around near a game booth and needs help finding family.

## Main Moral Value
Protection.

## Secondary Value
Responsibility.

## Suggested Setting
School fair.

## Main Character
Jonah, age 10, playful, strong-willed, and learning responsibility.

## Supporting Characters
Little Mina, booth helpers, Jonah's friends, Mina's parent.

## Natural Humour Idea
A beanbag toss announcer keeps giving very serious commentary for tiny throws.

## Moral Problem
Jonah can keep playing or help a smaller child who is scared.

## Resolution Direction
He shields the child from the crowd and gets adult help.
