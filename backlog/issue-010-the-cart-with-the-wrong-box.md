# Issue 010: The Cart With the Wrong Box

## Status
Draft Complete

## Inspiration Seed
Salamah ibn al-Akwa alertness: noticing danger, calling for help, and acting quickly but safely.

## Invisible Moral Pattern
A child spots a problem others miss and alerts adults instead of acting recklessly.

## Child-World Transformation
A charity donation box is accidentally loaded with recycling cartons and taken toward the driveway.

## Main Moral Value
Responsibility.

## Secondary Value
Alert courage.

## Suggested Setting
School driveway.

## Main Character
Kiran, age 10, fast-thinking, careful, and observant.

## Supporting Characters
Delivery worker, office staff, classmates.

## Natural Humour Idea
Kiran's shoelace keeps demanding attention at the worst possible time.

## Moral Problem
Kiran must decide whether to ignore the mistake or speak up and follow safely.

## Resolution Direction
Kiran alerts adults and helps recover the box without risky behavior.
