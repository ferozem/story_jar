# Issue 005: The Last Mango Slice

## Status
Draft Complete

## Inspiration Seed
Ansari guest: sharing generously even when there is little.

## Invisible Moral Pattern
A child gives up part of a favorite limited treat so someone else is not left out.

## Child-World Transformation
During a picnic, a new classmate forgets lunch and a child shares the snack he had been saving.

## Main Moral Value
Generosity.

## Secondary Value
Hospitality.

## Suggested Setting
Park picnic.

## Main Character
Arjun, age 9, food-loving, friendly, and quietly thoughtful.

## Supporting Characters
Meera, new classmate; Zoya, joke-loving friend; Mr. Finch, supervising adult.

## Natural Humour Idea
Arjun gives his lunchbox sections official names like treasure rooms.

## Moral Problem
Arjun wants to keep his favorite mango slices but sees Meera has nothing to eat.

## Resolution Direction
Arjun shares without making a show of it and learns that generosity can make a small lunch feel big.
