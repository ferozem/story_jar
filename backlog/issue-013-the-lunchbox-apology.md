# Issue 013: The Lunchbox Apology

## Status
Draft Complete

## Inspiration Seed
Abu Dharr corrected: learning humility after a hurtful word about background or family.

## Invisible Moral Pattern
A child realizes teasing someone's accent, clothes, or lunch is wrong and repairs the harm.

## Child-World Transformation
A child mocks a classmate's homemade lunch and later learns how hurtful the comment was.

## Main Moral Value
Respect.

## Secondary Value
Humility.

## Suggested Setting
Lunch area.

## Main Character
Rohan, age 9, funny, impulsive, and learning to think before joking.

## Supporting Characters
Nell, whose lunch is mocked; lunch friends; supervising auntie.

## Natural Humour Idea
Rohan's own sandwich falls apart after he acts too proud of it.

## Moral Problem
Rohan must decide whether to defend his joke or apologize sincerely.

## Resolution Direction
He apologizes, includes Nell, and learns that jokes are not funny when they make someone smaller.
