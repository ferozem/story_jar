# Issue 020: The Good Things List

## Status
Draft Complete

## Inspiration Seed
Khadijah comforting the Prophet: seeing and naming goodness when someone feels shaken.

## Invisible Moral Pattern
A loyal friend or sibling reminds a discouraged child of quiet good qualities.

## Child-World Transformation
A child fails badly in an activity and feels useless until someone lists the kind things he does.

## Main Moral Value
Encouragement.

## Secondary Value
Loyalty.

## Suggested Setting
Family home.

## Main Character
Benji, age 8, sensitive, helpful, and discouraged after failure.

## Supporting Characters
Older sister Lina, parent, neighborhood children.

## Natural Humour Idea
Lina's list includes "rescued the soup spoon from under the sofa," which Benji insists was serious work.

## Moral Problem
Benji wants to give up because one failure makes him forget his good qualities.

## Resolution Direction
Lina reminds him of his kindness and effort, helping him try again.
