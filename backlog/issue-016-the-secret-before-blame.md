# Issue 016: The Secret Before Blame

## Status
Draft Complete

## Inspiration Seed
Hatib ibn Abi Baltah: judging with context after a serious mistake.

## Invisible Moral Pattern
A child learns to ask and listen before accusing someone.

## Child-World Transformation
A team secret is shared, but the child who shared it was pressured and frightened.

## Main Moral Value
Fairness.

## Secondary Value
Mercy with accountability.

## Suggested Setting
Club room.

## Main Character
Nora, age 10, loyal, quick-tempered, and learning careful fairness.

## Supporting Characters
Sam, accused teammate; club members; older helper.

## Natural Humour Idea
A secret plan name is so silly nobody can say it without smiling.

## Moral Problem
Nora wants to call Sam a traitor before knowing the full story.

## Resolution Direction
She asks what happened, protects the team, and helps Sam make amends.
