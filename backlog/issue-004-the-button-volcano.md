## Status
Draft Complete

## Inspiration Seed
Anas serving the Prophet: long patience and kindness toward a younger helper who makes mistakes.

## Main Moral Value

## Main Character
Maya, age 10, creative, responsible, and sometimes impatient.

## Supporting Characters

## Natural Humour Idea
Rafi calls the button box a "button volcano" after it spills across the floor.

## Moral Problem
Maya wants to snap when Rafi keeps mixing up the craft supplies.

## Resolution Direction
Maya turns the work into a game and helps Rafi finish with confidence.
