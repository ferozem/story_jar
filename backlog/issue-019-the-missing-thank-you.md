# Issue 019: The Missing Thank-You

## Status
Draft Complete

## Inspiration Seed
Julaibib: no overlooked person should be forgotten.

## Invisible Moral Pattern
A child notices the quiet helper missing from praise and brings him forward.

## Child-World Transformation
After a school event, the performers are thanked but the child who arranged chairs is left out.

## Main Moral Value
Inclusion.

## Secondary Value
Dignity.

## Suggested Setting
School event.

## Main Character
Poppy, age 9, cheerful, observant, and fair-minded.

## Supporting Characters
Quiet boy Eli, performers, photographer, teacher.

## Natural Humour Idea
The group photo keeps being delayed because a banner slowly curls over one word.

## Moral Problem
Poppy can enjoy the applause or speak up for someone unseen.

## Resolution Direction
She asks for Eli to be included in the thank-you photo and thanks him by name.
