# Issue 001: The Trail Marker Promise

## Status
Draft Complete

## Inspiration Seed
Hijrah cave: calm courage and loyal reassurance while danger feels close.

## Invisible Moral Pattern
One companion stays calm when another is afraid, follows the safety plan, and reassures instead of panicking.

## Child-World Transformation
Two children are separated from a nature-trail group and must wait safely near a marked point.

## Main Moral Value
Calm friendship.

## Secondary Value
Trusting a good plan.

## Suggested Setting
Hill trail.

## Main Character
Nida, age 9, observant, steady, and kind under pressure.

## Supporting Characters
Omar, age 8, nervous but loyal; Ms. Reed, the trip leader; other classmates.

## Natural Humour Idea
Omar carries too many "emergency" snacks and discovers crackers in strange pockets.

## Moral Problem
Omar wants to run around searching, while Nida knows they should stay by the trail marker.

## Resolution Direction
Nida reassures Omar, uses the whistle correctly, and helps him wait until adults find them.
