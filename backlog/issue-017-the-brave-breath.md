# Issue 017: The Brave Breath

## Status
Draft Complete

## Inspiration Seed
Khubaib calm dignity in fear, adapted softly for children.

## Invisible Moral Pattern
A child faces fear with a quiet moment and dignified courage.

## Child-World Transformation
A child is frightened before a medical injection and chooses breathing and steadiness over blaming others.

## Main Moral Value
Inner courage.

## Secondary Value
Calmness.

## Suggested Setting
Clinic.

## Main Character
Zain, age 8, imaginative, worried, and able to be brave in a small real way.

## Supporting Characters
Nurse, parent, younger child in waiting room.

## Natural Humour Idea
Zain names the chair "Captain Squeak" because it makes a tiny noise.

## Moral Problem
Zain wants to run away and scare the younger child too.

## Resolution Direction
He takes a breath, faces the shot, and helps the younger child feel calmer.
