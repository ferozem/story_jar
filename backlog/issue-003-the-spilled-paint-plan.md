# Issue 003: The Spilled Paint Plan

## Status
Draft Complete

## Inspiration Seed
Bedouin mosque incident: gentle correction when a newcomer makes a serious mistake.

## Invisible Moral Pattern
People react harshly to a messy error, but one person calms the group and corrects with kindness.

## Child-World Transformation
A new student misunderstands poster instructions and spills paint over a shared class display.

## Main Moral Value
Gentleness.

## Secondary Value
Patience with newcomers.

## Suggested Setting
Art room.

## Main Character
Leela, age 8, practical, warm, and quick to notice when someone feels ashamed.

## Supporting Characters
Ben, the new child; Jay, impatient classmate; Ms. Coral, art teacher.

## Natural Humour Idea
A paint label gets read too literally, leading to a harmless "shake well" misunderstanding.

## Moral Problem
Leela must choose between joining the shouting or helping Ben fix the mistake.

## Resolution Direction
Leela asks everyone to stop, helps clean, and turns the ruined patch into a new poster idea.
