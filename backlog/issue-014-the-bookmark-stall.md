# Issue 014: The Bookmark Stall

## Status
Draft Complete

## Inspiration Seed
Abdur-Rahman ibn Awf: grateful self-reliance and dignity.

## Invisible Moral Pattern
A child accepts guidance but chooses effort instead of depending completely on gifts.

## Child-World Transformation
A child loses school supplies and starts a small bookmark stall at the class fair to earn replacements.

## Main Moral Value
Dignity.

## Secondary Value
Gratitude.

## Suggested Setting
Class fair.

## Main Character
Mina, age 10, artistic, proud in a healthy way, and hardworking.

## Supporting Characters
Helpful friend, fair visitors, parent volunteer.

## Natural Humour Idea
A bookmark shaped like a carrot sells faster than the fancy ones.

## Moral Problem
Mina could accept everything from a friend or work for what she needs with help.

## Resolution Direction
She asks for help setting up the stall and earns enough for supplies.
