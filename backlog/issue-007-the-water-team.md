# Issue 007: The Water Team

## Status
Draft Complete

## Inspiration Seed
Aishah and Umm Sulaym carrying water: brave service while others focus on the main event.

## Invisible Moral Pattern
A child chooses useful service over applause.

## Child-World Transformation
During a school race, one child notices younger students need help and starts a water-help team.

## Main Moral Value
Service.

## Secondary Value
Courageous action.

## Suggested Setting
School race.

## Main Character
Hana, age 10, quick, observant, and not afraid to leave the spotlight.

## Supporting Characters
Younger runners, a cheering crowd, Mr. Vale.

## Natural Humour Idea
Water cups get labeled with overly dramatic "speed fuel" signs.

## Moral Problem
Hana wants to be seen as a winner but sees a better way to help.

## Resolution Direction
She organizes classmates to serve water and earns respect through usefulness.
