# Issue 015: The Quiet Cleanup

## Status
Draft Complete

## Inspiration Seed
Umm Salamah at Hudaybiyyah: wise action when people are emotionally stuck.

## Invisible Moral Pattern
A child leads by starting the right action quietly instead of lecturing upset people.

## Child-World Transformation
A group loses a competition and refuses to help clean up afterward.

## Main Moral Value
Leadership by action.

## Secondary Value
Emotional maturity.

## Suggested Setting
Community hall.

## Main Character
Tessa, age 9, calm, observant, and quietly brave.

## Supporting Characters
Disappointed teammates, event helper, rival team.

## Natural Humour Idea
Someone tries to fold a tablecloth and turns it into a giant fabric snake.

## Moral Problem
Tessa can sulk with everyone or begin doing what needs to be done.

## Resolution Direction
She starts cleaning, and others join because action feels better than sulking.
