# Issue 006: The Finish-Line Water Bottle

## Status
Draft Complete

## Inspiration Seed
Water passed to others: selflessness while personally in need.

## Invisible Moral Pattern
Several tired children each think of someone else's thirst before their own.

## Child-World Transformation
During a hot sports day, one bottle remains near the finish line and tired runners keep offering it to each other until an adult brings more.

## Main Moral Value
Selflessness.

## Secondary Value
Compassion.

## Suggested Setting
Sports day.

## Main Character
Ivy, age 9, energetic, competitive, and able to notice others.

## Supporting Characters
Noah, Priya, Coach Lane, other runners.

## Natural Humour Idea
A tired runner tries to cheer and makes a squeaky whisper instead.

## Moral Problem
Ivy badly wants the last drink but sees others need it too.

## Resolution Direction
She offers it first, starting a chain of care that draws adult help.
