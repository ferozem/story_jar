# Issue 002: The Kindness Round

## Status
Draft Complete

## Inspiration Seed
Taif: mercy after being hurt instead of answering cruelty with cruelty.

## Invisible Moral Pattern
A child has power to embarrass someone who mocked him, but chooses mercy and quiet help.

## Child-World Transformation
A class presentation mocker later freezes before a contest round and needs help.

## Main Moral Value
Forgiveness.

## Secondary Value
Emotional strength.

## Suggested Setting
School contest.

## Main Character
Sami, age 10, thoughtful, shy in front of groups, stronger than he realizes.

## Supporting Characters
Milo, who mocked him; Anika, a practical friend; Mrs. Dutta, contest organizer.

## Natural Humour Idea
A child confidently mispronounces "finalists" as "fine-lists."

## Moral Problem
Sami can let Milo fail after being mocked or help him remember the rules.

## Resolution Direction
Sami quietly helps Milo, showing that forgiveness is stronger than payback.
