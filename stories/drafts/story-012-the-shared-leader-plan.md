# The Shared Leader Plan

## Story

Sofia's class was preparing a science exhibition about plants for visiting parents. Her group had grown beans in jars. Arman's group had made a model greenhouse from cardboard and clear plastic.

Both groups wanted the front table.

"Our beans are alive," Sofia said. "Visitors should see them first."

"Our greenhouse has a door that opens," Arman said, opening it three times to prove the point.

The door fell off on the fourth try.

"Temporary door," Arman said quickly.

By lunch, the two groups had made separate signs. Sofia's said MAIN PLANT TABLE. Arman's said MAIN GREENHOUSE TABLE. They both pointed to the same spot, which made the display look like it was arguing with itself.

Mrs. Lobo said, "You have ten minutes to decide."

Sofia wanted to be leader. She had watered the beans every day and reminded everyone not to shake the jars "to encourage them." She could insist. Her group would support her.

But she looked around. Arman's group was upset. Her own group was whispering angrily. No one was arranging the leaves, labels, or visitor cards.

If they kept fighting, the exhibition would look messy for everyone, and visitors would remember the arguing more than the plants.

Sofia took down her sign. "What if there is no main table?"

Arman frowned. "Then where does my temporary door go?"

"In the middle," Sofia said. "The beans can go on one side, the greenhouse on the other. We can take turns explaining. First visitor, your group starts. Next visitor, our group starts."

Her friend Priya whispered, "But you were going to lead."

Sofia swallowed. "I can lead one part. We do not need to fight over the whole thing."

Arman picked up the fallen door. "Shared table?"

"Shared table," Sofia said.

The last ten minutes became busy instead of noisy. They taped one sign above everything: HOW PLANTS GROW. When visitors came, the groups took turns. Sofia still explained the beans proudly, but she also showed people Arman's repaired door.

At the end, Mrs. Lobo said their display felt like one team had made it.

Sofia decided that felt better than one person being in charge.

## Moral of the Story

Peace sometimes begins when someone gives up pride so everyone can succeed together.
