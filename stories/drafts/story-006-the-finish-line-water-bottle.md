# The Finish-Line Water Bottle

## Story

Sports Day was so hot that even the flags beside the field looked tired. Ivy ran the final relay for Blue House, with her number pinned slightly crooked. She liked running because her feet seemed to know what to do before her brain finished thinking.

When the whistle blew, Ivy raced down the track. Noah from Green House was just ahead of her, and Priya was close behind. The crowd shouted, but by the last few steps, Ivy could only hear her own breath.

She crossed the finish line second and bent over, hands on knees.

"Water," Noah whispered.

"That was not a word," Priya said, flopping onto the grass. "That was a dusty frog noise."

Ivy laughed weakly. Then she saw the problem. The water crate near the finish line was empty except for one bottle that had rolled behind a cone.

Noah reached it first. His face was red, and sweat ran down his forehead. Ivy's mouth felt dry, like she had swallowed chalk dust.

Noah picked up the bottle, looked at Ivy and Priya, and paused.

"You drink," he told Priya. "You look like a tomato trying to become a raisin."

Priya took the bottle, then looked at Ivy. "You ran the last leg. You need it."

Ivy wanted to say yes. She wanted to drink half the bottle in one go. But Noah was still breathing hard, and Priya's hands were shaking a little.

"Noah first," Ivy said. "He finished before me."

Noah shook his head. "We can each take one sip."

They passed the bottle carefully. One sip for Noah. One for Priya. One for Ivy. It was not enough, but it helped.

Coach Lane hurried over with a full crate. "I wondered where the spare bottles went. They were at the long jump table."

Soon all three had proper drinks. Coach Lane smiled when he heard what happened. "You noticed each other even when you were tired. That matters."

Ivy did not win the relay, but as she walked back with Noah and Priya, she felt proud of their tiny circle around one bottle.

## Moral of the Story

Thinking of others, even when you need something too, is a brave and generous choice.
