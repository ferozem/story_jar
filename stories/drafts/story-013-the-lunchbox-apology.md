# The Lunchbox Apology

## Story

Rohan liked making people laugh. At lunch, he could turn a carrot stick into a microphone or make his juice box sound like a tiny trumpet. His friends usually saved him a seat because lunch felt brighter with him there. Most of the time, his jokes were harmless.

On Tuesday, Nell opened a round steel lunchbox. Inside was rice with vegetables and a sauce Rohan had never seen before.

"What is that smell?" he said, wrinkling his nose. "Did your lunch get lost in a garden?"

Two children laughed. Nell closed the lunchbox a little.

"It is from my grandma," she said.

Rohan grinned, but the grin did not feel as fun when Nell stopped eating. She pushed her spoon around without lifting it. He told himself it was only a joke.

Then he lifted his own sandwich. The bottom slice slipped away. Tomato, cheese, and lettuce slid onto his napkin in a slow, silent landslide.

Zara looked at it. "Your sandwich has resigned."

Everyone laughed, including Rohan. But when he looked at Nell, she was staring at her closed lunchbox.

The laugh faded from his chest.

After lunch, the class worked on a map project. Nell drew neat rivers and tiny trees. Rohan needed green pencil, and Nell had the best one. He almost asked as if nothing had happened.

Instead, he said, "I am sorry about what I said."

Nell kept drawing for a moment. "My grandma makes that food when she visits. I was excited to bring it."

Rohan's face grew hot. "I should not have made fun of it. I did not know it was special, but I still should not have said that."

Nell looked at him. "It is okay to ask what something is."

"Not okay to make a garden face?" Rohan asked.

Nell smiled a little. "Not okay."

The next day, Rohan sat beside her at lunch. He did not make a joke about the food. He asked what it was called. Nell told him, and Rohan practiced saying the name carefully.

His juice box made a tiny trumpet sound by accident. This time, everyone laughed together.

## Moral of the Story

Respect means not making fun of someone's food, family, accent, clothes, or background.
