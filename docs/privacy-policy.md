# Privacy Policy — Story Jar

**Last updated:** 15 July 2026

Story Jar ("the app", "we", "us") is a children's storytelling app that offers short
moral and character-building stories with illustrations and audio narration. We take
children's privacy seriously. This policy explains what we do — and, more importantly,
what we **don't** do — with data.

## The short version

**Story Jar does not collect, store, or share any personal information.** There are no
accounts, no login, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

## Information we do NOT collect

- No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contact details.
- No accounts or profiles — you never sign in.
- No advertising identifiers, and no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
- No analytics or usage-tracking tools.
- No location data.
- No microphone, camera, contacts, or photo access.

## Information stored on your device only

Your reading activity — such as which stories you've marked as favorites, your reading
progress, and which stories you've read — is stored **only on your own device**. It is
never transmitted to us or anyone else, and it is removed if you uninstall the app.

## How story content is delivered

Story text, images, and audio are delivered from a content-hosting service
(Cloudflare R2, a content delivery network) so the app can add new stories without a
software update. When the app downloads this content, the hosting provider may process
standard technical request information (such as an IP address) at the network level, as
happens with any internet request. We do not use this information to identify you, and we
do not receive or store it. See Cloudflare's privacy policy:
https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

## Children's privacy

Story Jar is designed for children and families. Because we collect no personal
information and require no accounts, the app is built to comply with children's privacy
regulations including the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the
"children" provisions of the EU/UK GDPR. We do not knowingly collect any data from
children or anyone else.

## Data sharing and selling

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any information, because we do not collect any.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post the
revised policy at this same address.
