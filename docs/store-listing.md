## Full description (max 4000 chars)
```
Story Jar is a cosy collection of short, meaningful stories that help children build
good character — honesty, kindness, courage, patience, gratitude, fairness, forgiveness,
humility, and generosity.

Each story is simple to read, set in everyday life, and ends with a gentle lesson worth
talking about. No fantasy filler, no scary content, no bad behaviour made to look cool —
just warm, real-life situations children can relate to, and values parents are happy to
share.

WHY FAMILIES LOVE STORY JAR

• Read or listen — every story has clear, soothing audio narration, perfect for bedtime
  or quiet time.
• Beautiful illustrations for every story.
• Organised by virtue — pick a "jar" like Kindness, Courage, or Honesty and open a story.
• Story of the Day — a fresh pick each day.
• Keep favorites and pick up where you left off.
• Talk-about lessons — each story ends with a simple takeaway to discuss together.

SAFE AND CALM BY DESIGN

• No ads. Ever.
• No sign-up and no accounts.
• No tracking and no data collection.
• No microphone, camera, or location access.

Story Jar was made to be the kind of app you can hand to your child without a second
thought — quiet, kind, and genuinely good for them.

Download Story Jar and start building character, one story at a time.
```
